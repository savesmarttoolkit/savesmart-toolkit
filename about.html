About SaveSmart Toolkit

SaveSmart Toolkit is a free collection of simple online calculators designed to help people understand everyday money decisions.

Our tools can help you compare product prices, plan a monthly budget, estimate debt payoff time, calculate loan payments, set savings goals, review subscriptions, calculate tips and explore long-term growth.

Our goal

Money decisions can feel confusing. Our goal is to make basic calculations faster and easier without requiring an account, spreadsheet or complicated financial software.

All calculations happen in your browser. We aim to provide practical educational estimates that can help you ask better questions and make more informed decisions.

Important disclaimer

SaveSmart Toolkit provides general educational information only. The results are estimates and are not financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Always verify important financial decisions and consider speaking with a qualified professional when appropriate.
