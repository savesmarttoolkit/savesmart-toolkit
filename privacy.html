Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

SaveSmart Toolkit provides free, browser-based calculators for everyday money decisions. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

You do not need to create an account to use SaveSmart Toolkit. We do not ask for your name, email address, payment details, or other personal information in order to use the calculators.

The numbers you enter into a calculator—such as income, expenses, debt balances, loan amounts, savings goals, or product prices—are processed directly in your browser. We do not store these entries on our servers.

How We Use Information

Calculator inputs are used only to generate an estimate on your device. When you refresh or close the page, the information you entered is not saved by SaveSmart Toolkit.

We may collect limited, automatic technical information to help operate and improve the website, such as browser type, device type, approximate location based on IP address, referring website, and pages visited.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies for basic site functionality, analytics, and advertising. Cookies are small files stored by your browser that can help websites remember preferences or understand how visitors use a site.

You can usually control or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you block cookies, some parts of the website may not function properly.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

If advertising or analytics services are used on this website, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-party services may collect information such as IP address, device information, browser information, and interaction data. Their use of that information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

SaveSmart Toolkit is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this website, please contact us so we can address the issue.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the website, but no method of transmission or storage over the Internet is completely secure. Because calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not stored by us, we do not maintain a database of your calculator entries.

External Links

This website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. Please review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the website after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the contact information provided on this website.
